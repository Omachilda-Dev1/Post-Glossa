## The Myth of the Language Genius

Polyglots aren't born — they're built. The people who speak 5, 8, or 12 languages aren't smarter than you. They've simply developed systems that make language acquisition inevitable rather than effortful.

### The Minimum Viable Practice

Research consistently shows that 20 minutes of daily practice outperforms 3-hour weekend sessions. The reason is consolidation — your brain processes and stores memories during sleep. Daily practice means daily consolidation.

```
Monday:    20 min Spanish vocabulary
Tuesday:   20 min Spanish listening
Wednesday: 20 min Spanish speaking (iTalki tutor)
Thursday:  20 min Spanish reading
Friday:    20 min Spanish writing
Weekend:   Review weak areas
```

### The Comprehensible Input Principle

Stephen Krashen's input hypothesis: you acquire language when you understand messages slightly above your current level (i+1). This means:

- Reading graded readers, not literary novels (at first)
- Watching TV with subtitles in the target language
- Listening to podcasts designed for learners

### Habit Stacking for Language Learning

Attach language practice to existing habits:

| Existing Habit | Language Addition |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Morning coffee | 10 Anki flashcards |
| Commute | Target language podcast |
| Lunch | Read one article |
| Evening walk | Shadowing audio |
| Before sleep | Review today's vocabulary |

### Managing Multiple Languages

The key to learning multiple languages simultaneously: never let a language go dormant. Even 5 minutes of maintenance prevents regression.

- **Active language** (currently learning): 20+ min/day
- **Maintenance language** (already learned): 5-10 min/day
- **Dormant language** (paused): monthly review session

### The Output Trap

Many learners consume endlessly but never produce. Speaking and writing are uncomfortable — and that discomfort is exactly where growth happens. Force output early, even when it's imperfect.

The goal isn't perfection. The goal is communication.
